# What $19.99 means

One digital copy of Arguments for An Abolishment of Corporate-Owned America, pen name Julian King. Buyer sends $19.99 on Venmo to @Alexander-Caro-31 with the note JULIAN KING BOOK. That note is the whole filing system. Without it, a payment is just money.

Do not invent a sales number. Start at zero copies. Count only what Venmo actually shows.

## Venmo to bank

The $19.99 lands in Venmo first. It is not in the bank until you transfer it.

Standard transfer to a linked bank: free. Typically 1 to 3 business days. Weekends and U.S. bank holidays do not count. A transfer started after the evening cutoff, or on a Saturday, starts its clock on the next business day.

Instant transfer: a fee. Venmo's current instant fee is 1.75 percent of the amount, minimum $0.25, maximum $25. On one $19.99 copy that is about $0.35. The money usually arrives in minutes. Use this only if you need the bank today. Waiting costs nothing.

A buyer who funds their Venmo send with a credit card may be charged Venmo's card fee on their side. That is their receipt, not yours. Receiving a standard person-to-person payment from a bank or Venmo balance does not take a cut of the $19.99.

Transfer when you want. Do not treat the Venmo balance as already-in-the-bank.

## How to log Paid on Sales

Personal Venmo has no incoming webhook. You are the webhook.

1. When someone hits Buy, a due copy appears on Sales. Amount is $19.99.
2. Open Venmo. Search activity for JULIAN KING BOOK.
3. If a $19.99 payment with that note is there, go back to Sales and tap Paid on that due copy.
4. If nothing matches, leave it due. If it was a misclick, tap Void.
5. Do not tap Paid because someone said they paid. Tap Paid when the Venmo line exists.

Honor system on the buyer's device (unlock) is not the same as Paid in your ledger. Unlock lets them read. Paid is your record that the money arrived.

## Do not mix tutoring money with book notes

Tutoring and the book share a Venmo handle. They must not share a note.

Tutoring payments keep tutoring notes: session, student, invoice number. Log those in Till, not as book copies.

Book payments must say JULIAN KING BOOK. Log those on Sales, not as tutoring slips.

If a parent sends $19.99 with no note, or with a session note, it is not a book copy until they correct the note or you have a written confirmation that the $19.99 was for the book. Ask once. Do not guess.

If someone sends tutoring money with the book note by mistake, fix the note with them before you tap Paid on Sales. A mixed line will wreck the weekly count.

## Weekly tally

Once a week, same day if you can:

1. Count Paid copies on Sales.
2. Count Venmo payments whose note is JULIAN KING BOOK and whose amount is $19.99 (or a clear multiple).
3. The two counts should match. If they do not, find the stray line before you transfer.
4. Write one line: date, number of copies, dollar total. Example shape, not a claim: 2026-09-14 · 0 copies · $0.00

Keep a list of copies: date, buyer if you know them, amount. That list is the record. Not tax advice. Just keep the list.
