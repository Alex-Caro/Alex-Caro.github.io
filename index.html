FAQ

Common questions

Straight answers before you reach out.

How long does a typical site take?

Most Basic sites ship in about 1-2 weeks. Business sites usually take 2-4 weeks depending on content and booking or shop setup. Custom apps are scoped per project.

Do you really do same-day fixes?

Yes, for scoped issues like copy updates, broken links, form bugs, or layout glitches. Text me with details and I will confirm timing and which tier ($75 / $150 / $200) fits.

Can I pay with Venmo?

Yes. Venmo works well for fixes and smaller projects. For larger builds we can split payment by milestone.

Who owns the code when we are done?

You do. I hand over the files, repo access, and hosting setup so you are never locked in.

How many revisions are included?

Each package includes two rounds of design revisions during the build. Extra rounds or post-launch changes can be billed as fixes or covered under the $100/mo maintenance plan.
